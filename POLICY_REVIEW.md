## Approved rules and controls

- Pro is a ₦5,000 one-time account upgrade. Refund requests within seven days go to support. Duplicate charges/non-delivery are reviewed separately. Refunds are manual, preserve applicable rights and can remove Pro access.
- Account deletion removes content/media and archives minimum financial references, amounts, dates and statuses without birthday content or card details. Pending payments, processing payouts and unsettled money block account/canvas deletion.
- Cleanup removes expired/used tokens, expired/revoked sessions, edit logs and settled provider payloads after 30 days. Closed-account financial records expire after seven years unless held. Commands, a protected endpoint and a daily workflow exist; production scheduling requires deployment.
- Providers are named: Cloudinary, Neon, Resend, Bachs, Paystack, Sentry, Vercel and Render. Processing can involve other countries including the US. Actual locations, contracts and safeguards depend on configured services. No unsupported log/backup deadline is promised.
- Sentry session replay is disabled; error/performance monitoring remains.
- Cash gifts have a 1.5% platform charge, provider processing fees and a 2% payout fee. Owner-only payout starts after reveal and settlement of outstanding checkouts. Pending/OTP/uncertain transfers reconcile against the original reference.
- New gift checkouts close when payout starts. An unresolved checkout blocks payout/deletion until support reconciles it. Unexpected late payments require manual support review; do not discard or falsely fail them.
- Gift refund requests require payment/pool review; recall after payout is not guaranteed. Duplicate/unauthorized charges and non-delivery are investigated with the provider. No automated refund deadline is promised.
